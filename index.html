About Me

Hi there! I am a (upcoming) 4th Year BS-MS student majoring in Physics at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Kolkata.

Currently, my broad interests are in equilibrium and non-equilibrium statistical mechanics and quantum many-body physics. I am interested in collective behaviour, critical phenomena, and emergent phenomena in interacting systems, as well as the dynamics of driven and open quantum systems.

In Summer 2025, I worked under the supervision of Dr. M. Padmanath at the Institute of Mathematical Sciences (IMSc) on a project focused on reducing variance (noise!) in large time (two--point) correlations in MC simulations of free scalar field theory. As part of this work, I explored simulations of the 2D/3D Ising model and aspects of universality and critical behaviour in statistical physics.

In Summer 2026, I will be joining the Harish-Chandra Research Institute (HRI) as a visiting student under the supervision of Sayan Choudhury to work on driven--dissipative quantum systems.
