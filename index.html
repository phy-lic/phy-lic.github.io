About Me

Hi there! I am a 2nd Year BS-MS student majoring in Physics at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Kolkata.

My academic journey is driven by a deep curiosity about how complex physical systems work from first principles. I spend most of my time studying theoretical physics and developing computational tools to model physical phenomena.

Currently, my primary research interests lie in the frontiers of astrophysics, cosmology, and the interstellar medium. I am particularly drawn to building theoretical models, developing numerical simulation tools, and understanding the underlying mechanics of these complex systems.
