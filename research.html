Research & Major Projects

Focusing on the nonequilibrium quantum dynamics of interacting lattice systems.

Driven Dissipative Many-Body Systems

Visiting Student (VSP 2026) | Harish-Chandra Research Institute (HRI)
Guide: Dr. Sayan Choudhury

Currently working on driven dissipative many body systems. This includes two primary sub-projects:

Project 1: Investigated the enhancement of the coherent/superfluid-like regime in a two-site disordered Bose-Hubbard model in the presence of interactions and finite temperature effects.
Project 2: Studied the regular-to-chaotic crossover in an interacting bosonic system using a kicked Bose-Hubbard dimer model.

Variance Reduction in Large Time Correlations in Scalar Field Theory

Summer Research Intern (SRP 2025) | Institute of Mathematical Sciences (IMSc)
Guide: Dr. Padmanath Madanagopalan

We studied the signal-to-noise problem in large-time correlation functions of lattice scalar field theory. Using the analytically known distribution of bilocal correlators, we implemented a variance-reduction method that improves the extraction of effective masses from Monte Carlo simulations.

Report

Theoretical Modelling of Gas Properties in Dark Matter Halos

Remote Project Student | Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Indore
Guide: Dr. Priyanka Singh

Worked on a review project focusing on theoretical modelling in cosmology:

• Reproduced the Komatsu–Seljak model of intracluster gas in dark matter halos. Derived universal gas density and temperature profiles from hydrostatic equilibrium assumption and a polytropic equation of state.
• Reproduced the Press–Schechter model of hierarchical structure formation and the resulting dark matter halo mass function, investigating its dependence on cosmological parameters and cosmic time.

Course Projects

Entanglement as a Resource

In this project, we review the conceptual foundations of quantum entanglement and its role as a nonclassical resource in communication and cryptography. We then demonstrate its practical applications through Superdense Coding, Quantum Teleportation and Quantum Key Distribution (BB84 and E91) using Qiskit and IBM Quantum hardware.
