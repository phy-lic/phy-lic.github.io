Selected Research & Projects

Regular-to-Chaotic Crossover in Kicked Bose-Hubbard Dimer

Analyzed the transition of regular integrable dynamics to quantum chaotic behavior in an interacting bosonic system using a kicked Bose-Hubbard dimer model.

Code  /  Report

Coherent Regime Enhancement in Disordered Bose-Hubbard Systems

Studied the localized-to-coherent/superfluid-like crossover phenomena within a two-site disordered Bose-Hubbard framework under the competitive interplay of on-site interactions and effects of finite-temperature on it.

Presentation

Variance Reduction in Large Time Correlations in Scalar Field Theory

We studied the signal-to-noise problem in large-time correlation functions of lattice scalar field theory. Using the analytically known distribution of bilocal correlators, we implemented a variance-reduction method that improves the extraction of effective masses from Monte Carlo simulations.

Code  /  Report

Theoretical Modelling of Gas Properties in Dark Matter Halos

Reproduced the Komatsu–Seljak model of intracluster gas in dark matter halos. Derived universal gas density and temperature profiles from hydrostatic equilibrium assumption and a polytropic equation of state. Additionally reproduced the Press–Schechter model of hierarchical structure formation and the resulting dark matter halo mass function. Investigated its dependence on cosmological parameters and cosmic time.

Report  /  Notes

Quantum Entanglement as a Resource

Term Paper for Advanced Quantum Mechanics course

In this project, we review the conceptual foundations of quantum entanglement and its role as a nonclassical resource in communication and cryptography. We then demonstrate its practical applications through Superdense Coding, Quantum Teleportation and Quantum Key Distribution (BB84 and E91) using Qiskit and IBM Quantum hardware.
